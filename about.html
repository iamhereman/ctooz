From Veriftools to CTOOZ: A New Beginning

For years, Veriftools was a trusted name for users requiring fake identification documents—whether to protect their privacy or to expedite tasks. Thousands of users relied on Veriftools daily due to its ease of use and the professional quality of the results it delivered. The platform provided digital templates or Photoshop files for passports, driver's licenses, national ID cards, credit cards, and utility bills (such as electricity or water bills) from various countries; users could input their names, photos, and details to download images of these forged documents in .jpg or .pdf formats.

As the community grew, so did the platform. At various stages of its journey, the service operated under domains such as veriftools.net, veriftools.org and veriftools.com. Documents generated by Veriftools were highly accurate and virtually flawless. However, the platform was also used for fraudulent activities, eventually drawing the attention of the Federal Bureau of Investigation and the Dutch National Police. Consequently, the government seized all of Veriftools' assets. Despite this, user demand persisted; the Veriftools community launched new domains like veriftools.pro, veriftools.cu and veriftools.vip to continue serving users, though these, too, were eventually seized by the government.

Ultimately, the developers decided to rethink the entire system rather than simply running another online website.

That decision led to the creation of a completely new version of the platform. Today, the project operates under a new identity— CTOOZ. The goal was not merely to change the name; the objective was to resolve the major issues users faced when utilizing online services.

Why Was Everything Rebuilt?

Veriftools often poses risks related to malware, phishing links, and fraud; uploading your real photos or private information there could have seriously compromised your security.

The previous online version worked well, but it had some shortcomings.

An online server was required to create every document.

Users needed an internet connection.

Separate payments had to be made for each document created.

Users faced compromises regarding document security and privacy.

As usage increased, both operating costs and user expenses rose.

Your files remain on your own computer throughout the entire generation process. Nothing needs to be uploaded before processing, giving you greater control over sensitive projects and personal data.
